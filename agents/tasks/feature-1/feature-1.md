1. Problem Statement
    Context: Currently, students must navigate deep into specific course hierarchies to submit work.

    The Pain Point: If a student is inspired to write or create media in a "flow state," the administrative overhead of finding the right assignment page in Canvas can be a distraction or lead to accidental late submissions.

2. Feature Vision
    Example: "The Canvas Studio provides a distraction-free 'Global Sandbox' where students can create content first and decide where to submit it second, streamlining the final step of the academic workflow."

3. User Personas & Use Cases
    The Creative Student: Wants to record a video or snap a photo of a project and send it to their "Art 101" assignment without clicking through five menus.

    The Last-Minute Submitter: Has an essay ready in their "Studio" and needs to submit it across three different open assignments for different classes quickly.

4. Key Functional Pillars
    Summary of the components:
        The Creator Space: A simple, clean editor for text and media.

        The Smart Selector: A unified dropdown that knows what assignments are due soon across all courses.

        The Safety Net: A confirmation step that summarizes assignment details to prevent "wrong file, wrong course" errors.

5. Success Metrics
    How will we know this feature is actually helping?

        Efficiency: Reduced time-to-submit (clicks/seconds) compared to standard navigation.

        Engagement: Number of submissions originating from the Studio vs. the Course Page.

        Error Rate: Frequency of students "canceling" in the confirmation modal (indicating the safety net caught a mistake).
